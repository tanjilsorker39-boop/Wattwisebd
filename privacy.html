Privacy

The short version: your data stays on your device. We collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. WattWise BD has no accounts, no sign-in, no server that receives your bill, appliances, or personal information.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — monthly bills, appliance lists, savings goals, solar assumptions, language, and theme — is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. It never leaves your phone or computer.

Bill image

If you choose to upload a bill image for reference, it is only previewed in the page for your own viewing. It is not uploaded, not stored, not transmitted anywhere. Closing the tab discards it.

Clearing your data

You can wipe everything at any time:

  • Click "Clear all saved data" in the app.
  • Or clear your browser's site data for this website.

Analytics

WattWise BD does not currently run any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future (e.g. Plausible or Umami), this page will be updated first, and the tool will remain cookie-free.

Third parties

The only external requests this site makes are:

  • Google Fonts — to load typography.
  • jsDelivr CDN — to load the Chart.js library for the charts.

Those requests go directly from your browser to those services. See their own privacy pages for details.

Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13. Since no data is collected, no personal information is processed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date below will change. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage.
